Sleepy

20th August 2026

Went to the office. The B train was delayed in the morning. It was a bit chilly for August. Not much to mention at work though the negotiation I've been having now seems to be moving forward. Many people asked me about decision to going back to Japan and my trip to Sarajevo. People are also a bit worried about the investor change, it's interesting to observe how different people react differently. Just after the work, visited the camera store to develop my five films. Bought a pack of films for the portrait as well. There was a big noise from the construction and the inside was oddly warm, fan was shaking its head, which I've never seen in Denmark. Took a bus to receive two bags of coffee at Norange. Had pasta for dinner and walked to the cemetery which Kierkegaard is resting. Joined the football training from 8PM and arrived at home before 11. Now I had a little snack and getting sleepy. Bill Evans på Louisiana 24 04 1975
